# RETORIA HOME — product brief

RETORIA HOME is a premium home-textile storefront for customers who want a calm, tactile interior without the uncertainty of buying textiles online.

The prototype covers the complete customer path: discover the brand, browse and filter the catalog, configure a curtain, preview it in a room with an AI try-on, read reviews, add products, complete delivery and payment, and review the order confirmation.

Core pages: home, catalog, product, AI try-on, reviews, cart, delivery, payment, order confirmation, and contacts/brand information.

The prototype is intentionally front-end only. Cart, filters, forms, upload preview, comparison slider, and checkout navigation work locally; payment and AI generation are simulated clearly and never presented as completed external services.
