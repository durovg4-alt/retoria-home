# RETORIA HOME — design direction

## Character

Quiet luxury, tactile natural textiles, warm daylight, editorial composition. The experience should feel spacious and expensive, never crowded or marketplace-noisy.

## System

- Warm ivory canvas, oatmeal surfaces, muted moss actions, charcoal type.
- High-contrast Bodoni display typography paired with Manrope for interface text.
- Twelve-column desktop grid with a 24px gutter and a fluid 24–72px outer margin.
- Large sections use 112–152px vertical rhythm; smaller commerce surfaces use 24–40px.
- Rounded corners are restrained: 28–34px for feature imagery, 18–24px for cards, pill only for compact controls.
- Photography is warm, low-contrast, softly sunlit, and materially focused.

## Interaction

Motion is calm and useful: 180–360ms interface transitions, longer 700–1000ms editorial entrances, subtle image scale, and short staggered reveals. Lenis provides gentle smooth scrolling; GSAP handles route and scroll choreography. Reduced-motion preference removes non-essential animation.

## Content rules

One primary message per section. Short Russian copy. No redundant newsletter or benefit strips inside purchase flows. The AI try-on is the main differentiator and appears after product specifications.
